## Key Highlights

### 1. Engine-Level Optimization & Modifications
* **Precision Gunplay:** Features high-precision hitbox and hurtbox calculations.
* **Advanced Animation:** Smoother weapon animation handling with modern recoil dynamics.
* **Scripting Flexibility:** Integrates Pawn/Lua support for efficient entity management and environmental interaction.

### 2. Enhanced Performance & Multi-Platform Support
* Restructured architecture ensures stable, high frame rates during intense battles.
* Provides advanced rendering capabilities, improved texture filtering, and real-time lighting management.

### 3. Diverse & Cutting-Edge Game Modes
* Deep survival systems featuring advanced zombie mechanics.
* Tactical assault scenarios and fast-paced deathmatch arenas designed to test player reflexes.
